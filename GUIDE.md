# How to use ORION

Public cruise research. No booking transactions.

## Start with a place. Keep the particulars.

Search a destination, ship or itinerary. Compare exact departures, then keep the evidence and questions together.

### Start with your plans

Search a destination, ship, itinerary or agent name. Choose several destinations or ships. Use Filters to enter a trip-length range, exact duration or budget. Compare sellers selects departures with usable current agent advertisements.

### Understand the price in a glance

The card names the source of its lowest recorded guide for two. Current source-checked agent prices remain visible even when the supplier reference is lower. Open Cabins & sellers for the dated adverts and separate supplier categories.

### Compare and shortlist

Save your favourites and compare up to three voyages. Compare cabins and sellers, then copy a detailed quote request. The site does not submit that request or make a reservation.

### Check the dates

Price updates explains the date of the main fare feed, seller checks and this website edition. Automatic publication remains off. Earlier prices are supporting context and stay separate from recent supplier observations.

## Read the figure and its basis

A guide is a starting point. Its currency, party size, category and observation date tell you what it can support.

### Published guide for two

The default guide selects the lowest supplier reference or eligible agent advert. Agent advertisements must have exact product attribution checked against retained source evidence and an observation no older than 24 hours. An explicit two-person amount takes precedence; otherwise ORION doubles the per-person amount and labels the estimate. Selecting a supplier category restricts the guide and budget to that exact column.

### Cabin columns

The supplier combines codes such as SP/OV or S/B/IN across ships. ORION keeps those literal codes. Agent adverts retain their own cabin and package wording, with no inferred equivalence to a supplier category. Confirm the actual cabin grade and inclusions for your exact departure.

### Exact amounts and rounding

Cards, departure details, comparisons and question drafts retain pounds and pence. Chart axes may use whole-pound tick labels; the observation tables retain exact amounts. A seller's explicit two-person quote takes precedence over doubling its rounded per-person headline.

### Unknown does not mean zero

Taxes, port charges, gratuities, agency fees, flights and transfers may be included, excluded or unknown. Unknown amounts are not treated as free. Published All-Inclusive wording is a lead to verify for the exact fare.

### Supplier categories and duration

Category labels such as Y or SP/OV are the supplier’s published codes. ORION preserves them for comparisons within the same column. Confirm the cabin grade for your exact departure with the seller. Duration keeps its source basis: published supplier days or explicitly advertised nights. Estimated end dates are identified separately.

## What this index contains

Supplier products and individually reviewed publisher listings, with dated price evidence. Cabin availability requires confirmation.

### Products and departures

A ship and departure date may appear as a short cruise, a longer combined itinerary and a cruise-tour package. Those are distinct products. Counts should not be read as the number of unique physical sailings or available rooms.

### Days and nights

The official feed labels duration as Days. Reviewed publisher pages may explicitly state nights. Each record keeps that source basis. A package can include time before or after the cruise. Supplemental bundle listings keep the advertised start and nights, with the full package end date unverified. Searches with a return-by date exclude a package whose full end date is unknown.

### Coverage

ORION reconciles the complete saved supplier feed and adds individually reviewed publisher listings. The coverage counts identify both groups. Advertisements keep their own package terms and observation dates. Agency allocations, unpublished fares and newly added links can still fall outside that coverage.

### See more voyages without paging

ORION opens 96 results at a time. At the bottom of the results, choose 48, 96, 240 or All matching voyages. Your choice stays in this browser. Photos load as you reach them; list view is useful for a large comparison. The full matching count stays visible.

### Itineraries and route maps

Itinerary & route keeps the departure page within reach. Where reviewed stop facts are retained, ORION shows them with source dates and links. Elsewhere, open the full itinerary on the exact publisher page. External maps are seller illustrations and are not verified navigational tracks. Stops and timings can change.

## Make room for the right departure

Choose several ships or destinations and your complete travel window. Check the result count before applying.

### Several ships or regions

Tick any combination. Choices within ships or destinations are alternatives: a result can match any selected ship and any selected region. Your date, length and budget conditions must also match. Leaving a group clear includes everything in that group.

### Your whole cruise must fit

Earliest departure means the cruise starts on or after that date. Cruise ends by means it finishes on or before that date, inclusive. For 27 March to 18 April, a cruise finishing on 18 April is included; one finishing on 19 April is excluded, even if it leaves within the window. When no matched seller end date is retained, ORION conservatively estimates departure plus the published duration and labels it as estimated. Unknown or conflicting end dates cannot pass an end-date limit. Flights and transfers may need extra days.

### Your own trip length

Enter the minimum and maximum number of days, such as 9 to 12. Enter the same number twice for an exact length. Shortcuts are optional. The length follows the publisher’s listed trip duration; confirm actual cruise nights with your seller.

### Any budget

Type any minimum or maximum guide for two. The default uses the lowest eligible supplier or agent guide, with its source visible. A selected supplier category uses only that category. These amounts may exclude flights, hotels, transfers and required charges. Zero finds no positive-priced cruises; blank means no limit.

### Preview, apply or cancel

Show voyages gives the matching count while you choose. Apply once ready. Closing the panel leaves your previous filters unchanged. The chips above the results remove individual ships, regions or ranges without clearing everything else.

### Keep or share your view

Your selected ships, regions and ranges are stored in the page link. Reloading or sharing that link preserves the same filters. Saved voyages stay local to this browser.

## Compare the dates, then the particulars

A month can contain very different voyages. The calendar and an itinerary’s dates answer different questions.

### Price calendar

Each calendar bar shows the cheapest guide among cruises that both start and finish within that month and meet your other active filters. Selecting a month applies that whole-cruise date window. Itineraries, durations and cabins can differ, so this is a discovery tool.

### All dates for one voyage

Open All dates to compare departures with the same ship, itinerary, duration and type. With a date filter applied, only cruises that start and end within your window are shown. The chart, table, cheaper-date suggestions and grouped itinerary results respect those same bounds. Both departure and end dates appear, with calculated ends marked as estimates.

### A lower-priced date is not a discount

Moving to another date changes the purchase. Compare flights, time off and the full trip cost before deciding that the cheaper departure is better value.

## What changed, and when?

Choose a supplier category, seller observation series or archived capture. Each keeps its own price basis and original timestamp.

### A useful first read

At its lowest recorded price means the latest value equals the lowest among the observations held by ORION. It does not mean the lowest price ever offered or the cheapest available seller. Expand the details for exact values, dates and the supplier’s category code.

### Same column, actual observations

Select a supplier column in Price history. The chart plots only saved timestamp-and-price pairs for that exact product and column. A stepped line joins observations; it does not establish prices between checks. The data table exposes each plotted value.

### Seller observations

Opening history from an agent-led price selects that exact source observation and its seller, cabin and package series. The chart and main table include only observations with validated source attribution. Withdrawn or unresolved values remain in an audit disclosure with their original amounts, dates and correction references. Every retained observation stays accessible.

### A drop can apply to another cabin

A card's recorded-drop badge uses the largest decrease across that product's columns unless you filter to a particular column. Open the history to see the exact code, previous amount and current amount. Do not subtract that percentage from the lowest headline price.

### Limited history

History starts when ORION first collected the price. Five readings over a few days are not a 90-day benchmark. With fewer than two valid observations, ORION shows an insufficient-history message instead of inventing a trend.

### Latest successfully observed

Source validation establishes which published advert was observed for a product. It does not confirm current cabin availability or final cost. Current discovery excludes agent adverts older than 24 hours; their dated historical evidence remains accessible.

### Source records awaiting an exact match

The history coverage panel links to a download of seller records awaiting an unambiguous cruise match. Each keeps its original seller identity, date, duration, cabin, price basis and observations. Use its source link to check the commercial product before comparing prices.

## Similar dates are a starting point

Open the exact sailing from its price panel or the visible voyage actions. Read the current seller page before relying on an observed guide.

### Open the real sailing page

View sailing & current prices goes to an observed dated publisher or seller page, not a search engine or homepage. Publisher IDs and dates are matched; a different title is flagged because package wording can vary. The supplier index confirms the link was listed, not that every page was individually fetched or that a cabin is available. The seller page has its own current price and booking or enquiry options.

### Match the important fields

Match the ship, departure, cruise nights, cabin grade, occupancy, inclusions, taxes, required fees, cancellation conditions, payment currency and UK residency eligibility. Preserve a seller's explicit two-adult total when supplied.

### Dated advertising, not live checkout

Current agent adverts appear first with provider, category, per-person amount, pair basis and original observation time. They require exact source attribution and an observation within 24 hours. The separate retained-evidence disclosure preserves withdrawn, unresolved, conflicted and older records; these cannot determine the current guide or budget.

### Shared brands count once

Windstar UK and The Cruise Line are treated as one provider group. Iglu and Planet are one group. Multiple pages from the same group do not independently confirm a market saving.

### A useful next step

Copy quote request preserves the selected guide, provider, observation date and source-specific category. A supplier reference remains separate. Unknown cabin equivalence and package terms are explicit questions. The draft stays local until you choose how to use it.

## What would prove a saving?

A percentage is only useful when the underlying purchases are genuinely comparable.

### Research lead

A supplier price reduction, a lower advert, an offer page or an unusually cheap departure is a lead. It may still have the wrong cabin, excluded benefits, stale availability or market restrictions.

### SILVER and GOLD

SILVER requires at least 25% and GOLD at least 40% below a fresh, independent, equivalent UK booking price. The exact cabin must be available, all mandatory costs established and the booking route usable. An unresolved lower competitor prevents the claim.

### Nothing qualified yet

An empty Verified view is intentional when those checks have not passed. You can still explore the full catalogue and promising leads without being told that an unproven saving is ready to book.

## Keep a shortlist, keep the questions

Save exact departures on this browser. Compare them and prepare a local question draft to clarify the remaining differences.

### Save a voyage

Use the bookmark button on a card or in the voyage detail. Saved voyages appear under Saved. They are kept in this browser only. Clearing site storage removes them; they do not sync to another device.

### Compare up to three

Select Compare on up to three voyages, then open the comparison tray. Review dates, duration, price guide and evidence side by side. Different voyages and packages remain different products; their price difference is not automatically a saving.

### Export for your records

Export saved creates a CSV with each selected guide, provider or supplier category, original observation time, source link and price basis. It is a research snapshot. Your active supplier-category filter is preserved. Export before clearing saved voyages.

### Bookmarks and price watches are separate

Saving a voyage creates a bookmark. Track supplier names the actual supplier category used for a price target, including when an agent leads the card. Agent lead-ins do not create supplier-category watches. Watches check this browser while the website is open.

### Turn a comparison into questions

Choose up to three departures and open their comparison. Prepare comparison questions to keep the exact IDs, dates, observed categories and price basis in an editable draft. The draft is local, is not stored and sends nothing. Use Copy questions or select the text if clipboard access is unavailable. Confirm the cabin, availability, final GBP costs and inclusions directly with a seller.

## Read the benefit and the conditions

Check the dates, eligibility and exclusions for the exact departure. A public promotion does not establish entitlement.

### Four separate states

Offer cards show campaign status, evidence quality, personal eligibility and the transaction stage reached. Published does not mean quoted, and active does not mean available to a UK resident.

### Do not add discounts automatically

Your agent quote may already include loyalty or All-Inclusive benefits. Onboard credit is different from cash. Deposits affect cash flow, not necessarily the overall fare. Combining offers requires explicit terms for the same booking.

### Expired and excluded examples

The default list focuses on routes needing verification. Excluded examples can be revealed to explain regional restrictions or expired campaigns. No private account, invitation token or restricted document is exposed here.

## Two clocks: the evidence and the edition

A source check and a website release are separate events. Both dates matter when deciding what to investigate.

### Main cruise-price feed

The local collector runs hourly, but the main Windstar price feed currently has a 20-hour minimum between checks. A run before that deadline leaves the last successful price check unchanged. The next permitted check is shown under Price updates. Actual checks also depend on the computer being online and the source responding.

### Seller-price checks

Agencies have their own collection schedules. Each source-checked advertisement keeps its original observation time, including when saved source documents are reviewed later. Only observations no older than 24 hours contribute to current agent guides. Older evidence remains in dated history and audit tables.

### Publishing the website

Automatic website publication is not enabled. The collected data must be built, checked and published manually. The website edition date can therefore be newer than the fare data, or older than unpublished collected data.

### What reloading does

Reloading opens the newest edition already on GitHub Pages. It does not run collection, bypass a source interval or confirm cabin availability. Review the price and seller dates before asking for a current quote.

### An open page can notice a newer edition

While this tab is visible, ORION checks the published website edition every five minutes and when you return to the tab. A newly published data file is loaded while preserving your filter choices. This does not contact a cruise seller or bypass the separate publication step.

## Your research stays with you

Saved choices stay in this browser. ORION does not take bookings or send a seller the drafts you prepare.

### What is public

Only selected public supplier, seller and offer data is published. The private research database, existing reservations, account details, personal correspondence and credentials are not part of this site.

### Browser storage

Saved voyages, comparison choices and appearance preferences use your browser's local storage. The site does not send these choices to ORION or an agency. They are not encrypted backups; use a private device and export anything you need to retain.

### Hosting and external sites

GitHub Pages hosts this public website and records visitor IP addresses for security. ORION keeps your saved choices in this browser. Opening an external supplier or seller link takes you to a separate site with its own privacy practices.

### Keyboard and touch

Press / for search when outside a text field. Press Escape to close a dialog or tip. Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls. Contextual explanations also open by tapping the information button, so they do not depend on hover. Charts have readable data tables.

### Appearance and motion

Use the appearance control for system, light or dark mode. Reduced-motion settings are respected. No information depends on animation or colour alone.

## An earlier figure is useful context

An archived advertisement records what a seller published on one day. Gaps remain gaps; it is not today’s price.

### Start with the same voyage

ORION matches archived adverts to an exact retained product using the voyage identifier, ship, departure and duration. A different flight package, tour or duration requires its own match.

### Keep sources and cabin types separate

Choose Archived seller adverts in Price history. Each seller and cabin category has its own series. An unspecified lead-in fare cannot be treated as the price of your chosen cabin.

### Read the markers and source links

Diamond markers show real archive captures. Each table row opens the timestamped archived page. Capture time and retrieval time are different dates. Empty months have no evidence, and no prices are interpolated.

### Choose a useful time window

Use 30 days, 90 days, 12 months or All found. An empty range offers a way back to the full evidence. Lowest observed means the lowest captured price, not a guaranteed annual low.

### Use the evidence in a current quote

Ask a seller to confirm the exact cabin, inclusions and final UK total. An archived reduction alone cannot trigger a verified-bargain badge or establish current availability.

## Track an exact price

Choose a departure and supplier category, then save a target for two. Tracked prices stay in this browser.

### Which prices support a watch

A watch tracks one literal supplier category for an exact departure. Its button, selected category and current amount identify that supplier basis. An agent-led headline can have a different amount; it is never silently used as a supplier watch target. Agent adverts remain available for comparison and saved research.

### Save now, check when evidence is ready

An older observation can still support a saved target. The target remains pending until a fresh confirmed price check is published. That first fresh value establishes the baseline; it is not reported as a new bargain.

### Choose what matters

Set a target amount or open the optional percentage-decrease rule. The 5% and 10% buttons calculate a target from the selected category. If you explicitly set both conditions, either can create a notice. Different categories are tracked separately.

### A notice needs a new observation

Once a target is active, ORION checks newly published values for the same voyage and category. An unchanged or repeated observation does not create another notice. A matching target does not confirm availability, inclusions or a final quote.

### Where notices appear

Checks work while this page is open. Notices appear in Tracked prices. Browser desktop notices require permission and browser support. Email and closed-page push are not connected; closing the page stops these checks. Publication of new website editions remains manual.

### Keep control

Edit, pause or remove targets from Tracked prices. Your choices remain in this browser and are lost if you clear its site storage. Use the exact seller link to check the current quote or booking options.
