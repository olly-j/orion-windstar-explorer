# How to use ORION

Public cruise research. No booking transactions.

## Find a cruise worth a closer look

Choose the places and ships you like, compare prices and dates, then save the voyages worth checking.

### Start with your plans

Choose several destinations or ships. Use Filters to enter any trip-length range, exact duration or budget. You can see the matching count before applying.

### Understand the price in a glance

Open a voyage for its price guide, a graph of recorded prices and any lower-priced alternative dates. Estimated costs are for two people. The source and exact category are available under Price basis, source and dates.

### Compare and shortlist

Save your favourites and compare up to three voyages. Compare cabins and sellers, then copy a detailed quote request. The site does not submit that request or make a reservation.

### Check the dates

Price updates explains the date of the main fare feed, seller checks and this website edition. Automatic publication remains off. Earlier prices are supporting context and stay separate from recent supplier observations.

## What the price means

Most amounts on ORION are research guides. The wording beside the price is part of the evidence.

### Estimated cruise price for two

The headline guide is twice the lowest positive GBP cell in the supplier feed, or twice your selected column. This arithmetic does not prove the cell's occupancy basis, charges, availability or final payable amount. It does not automatically include flights, hotels or transfers.

### Cabin columns

The supplier combines codes such as SP/OV or S/B/IN across ships. ORION keeps those literal codes to avoid promising the wrong room. In Cabins & sellers, check the advertised grade against the actual ship and ask the agent to confirm it in writing.

### Exact amounts and rounding

Cards round guides to whole pounds for scanning. The detail tables and quote brief retain pounds and pence. A seller's explicit two-person quote must take precedence over doubling its rounded headline.

### Unknown does not mean zero

Taxes, port charges, gratuities, agency fees, flights and transfers may be included, excluded or unknown. Unknown amounts are not treated as free. Published All-Inclusive wording is a lead to verify for the exact fare.

## What is in the catalogue?

The catalogue contains published supplier products, not an inventory of available cabins.

### Products and departures

A ship and departure date may appear as a short cruise, a longer combined itinerary and a cruise-tour package. Those are distinct products. Counts should not be read as the number of unique physical sailings or available rooms.

### Days and nights

The official source labels duration as Days. ORION uses published days until the cruise nights are independently confirmed. A package can include time before or after the cruise.

### Coverage

The saved feed provides broad Windstar coverage within its published date range. It is not proof that every agency allocation, future release or invitation-only fare has been discovered. Missing prices and missing seller evidence are shown as gaps.

## Choose exactly what suits you

Select several ships or destinations, and enter your own trip length and budget. The preview shows how many voyages match before you apply.

### Several ships or regions

Tick any combination. Choices within ships or destinations are alternatives: a result can match any selected ship and any selected region. Your date, length and budget conditions must also match. Leaving a group clear includes everything in that group.

### Your own trip length

Enter the minimum and maximum number of days, such as 9 to 12. Enter the same number twice for an exact length. Shortcuts are optional. The length follows the publisher’s listed trip duration; confirm actual cruise nights with your seller.

### Any budget

Type any minimum or maximum price for two. These are cruise-only estimates and may exclude flights, transfers and mandatory charges. A maximum of zero finds no positive-priced cruises; a blank field means no limit.

### Preview, apply or cancel

Show voyages gives the matching count while you choose. Apply once ready. Closing the panel leaves your previous filters unchanged. The chips above the results remove individual ships, regions or ranges without clearing everything else.

### Keep or share your view

Your selected ships, regions and ranges are stored in the page link. Reloading or sharing that link preserves the same filters. Saved voyages stay local to this browser.

## Choose dates with price context

There are two different ways to compare dates, and each answers a different question.

### Price calendar

The calendar above the results shows the cheapest guide among the matching departures in each month of the selected year. Clicking a bar filters to that month. The underlying itineraries, durations and cabin columns can differ; this is a discovery tool.

### All dates for one voyage

Open All dates on a card to compare products with the same ship, named itinerary, published duration and type. The chart and table contain actual retained dates and guides. Select a date to inspect that product's own seller evidence.

### A lower-priced date is not a discount

Moving to another date changes the purchase. Compare flights, time off and the full trip cost before deciding that the cheaper departure is better value.

## Read recorded price changes correctly

A voyage’s overview shows how its selected price has moved. The graph uses actual checks of the same listed category, with clear dates and estimates for two.

### A useful first read

At its lowest recorded price means the latest value equals the lowest among the observations held by ORION. It does not mean the lowest price ever offered or the cheapest available seller. Expand the details for exact values, dates and the supplier’s category code.

### Same column, actual observations

Select a supplier column in Price history. The chart plots only saved timestamp-and-price pairs for that exact product and column. A stepped line joins observations; it does not establish prices between checks. The data table exposes each plotted value.

### A drop can apply to another cabin

A card's recorded-drop badge uses the largest decrease across that product's columns unless you filter to a particular column. Open the history to see the exact code, previous amount and current amount. Do not subtract that percentage from the lowest headline price.

### Limited history

History starts when ORION first collected the price. Five readings over a few days are not a 90-day benchmark. With fewer than two valid observations, ORION shows an insufficient-history message instead of inventing a trend.

### Latest successfully observed

A previous price can change again before you book. Source timestamps and any stale-data warning remain visible. A low raw fare still needs a current UK quote.

## Compare seller advertisements

The same departure is a useful starting point. An equivalent purchase needs more checks.

### Match the important fields

Match the ship, departure, cruise nights, cabin grade, occupancy, inclusions, taxes, required fees, cancellation conditions, payment currency and UK residency eligibility. Preserve a seller's explicit two-adult total when supplied.

### Dated advertising, not live checkout

The tables show when the advert was observed, its price surface and any conflicts. Rows marked Unverified are not confirmed available quotes. Visit the source or request a written quotation before acting.

### Shared brands count once

Windstar UK and The Cruise Line are treated as one provider group. Iglu and Planet are one group. Multiple pages from the same group do not independently confirm a market saving.

### A useful next step

Copy quote request creates a concise brief with the exact product, your selected supplier column and the questions still unanswered. The website does not submit it or transmit your details.

## What qualifies as a verified bargain?

A large marketing percentage is not enough.

### Research lead

A supplier price reduction, a lower advert, an offer page or an unusually cheap departure is a lead. It may still have the wrong cabin, excluded benefits, stale availability or market restrictions.

### SILVER and GOLD

SILVER requires at least 25% and GOLD at least 40% below a fresh, independent, equivalent UK booking price. The exact cabin must be available, all mandatory costs established and the booking route usable. An unresolved lower competitor prevents the claim.

### Nothing qualified yet

An empty Verified view is intentional when those checks have not passed. You can still explore the full catalogue and promising leads without being told that an unproven saving is ready to book.

## Save, compare and export a shortlist

Keep the voyages you are considering in one place.

### Save a voyage

Use the bookmark button on a card or in the voyage detail. Saved voyages appear under Saved. They are kept in this browser only. Clearing site storage removes them; they do not sync to another device.

### Compare up to three

Select Compare on up to three voyages, then open the comparison tray. Review dates, duration, price guide and evidence side by side. Different voyages and packages remain different products; their price difference is not automatically a saving.

### Export for your records

Export saved creates a CSV of the retained public values and source dates. It is a snapshot for research, not a quote. Export it before clearing saved voyages. Saving or comparing does not subscribe you to price alerts.

## Check benefits before combining them

A campaign's existence and your entitlement are separate facts.

### Four separate states

Offer cards show campaign status, evidence quality, personal eligibility and the transaction stage reached. Published does not mean quoted, and active does not mean available to a UK resident.

### Do not add discounts automatically

Your agent quote may already include loyalty or All-Inclusive benefits. Onboard credit is different from cash. Deposits affect cash flow, not necessarily the overall fare. Combining offers requires explicit terms for the same booking.

### Expired and excluded examples

The default list focuses on routes needing verification. Excluded examples can be revealed to explain regional restrictions or expired campaigns. No private account, invitation token or restricted document is exposed here.

## When do prices update?

Collection and website publication are separate. The website shows the latest published edition, with the actual dates of its prices.

### Main cruise-price feed

The local collector runs hourly, but the main Windstar price feed currently has a 20-hour minimum between checks. A run before that deadline leaves the last successful price check unchanged. The next permitted check is shown under Price updates. Actual checks also depend on the computer being online and the source responding.

### Seller-price checks

Agencies have their own check schedules. Their latest retained observation can be newer than the main cruise-price feed. Individual advertisements keep their own dates.

### Publishing the website

Automatic website publication is not enabled. The collected data must be built, checked and published manually. The website edition date can therefore be newer than the fare data, or older than unpublished collected data.

### What reloading does

Reloading opens the newest edition already on GitHub Pages. It does not run collection, bypass a source interval or confirm cabin availability. Review the price and seller dates before asking for a current quote.

## Privacy, accessibility and controls

The website is public. Your saved choices stay on this device.

### What is public

Only selected public supplier, seller and offer data is published. The private research database, existing reservations, account details, personal correspondence and credentials are not part of this site.

### Browser storage

Saved voyages, comparison choices and appearance preferences use your browser's local storage. The site does not send these choices to ORION or an agency. They are not encrypted backups; use a private device and export anything you need to retain.

### Keyboard and touch

Press / for search when outside a text field. Press Escape to close a dialog or tip. Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls. Contextual explanations also open by tapping the information button, so they do not depend on hover. Charts have readable data tables.

### Appearance and motion

Use the appearance control for system, light or dark mode. Reduced-motion settings are respected. No information depends on animation or colour alone.

## Use archived prices without guessing

An archived price tells you what a seller published on a particular day. It adds context to today’s research.

### Start with the same voyage

ORION accepts archived adverts only when the voyage identifier, ship, departure and duration match a current product. Flight packages and tours with different durations are excluded.

### Keep sources and cabin types separate

Choose Archived seller adverts in Price history. Each seller and cabin category has its own series. An unspecified lead-in fare cannot be treated as the price of your chosen cabin.

### Read the markers and source links

Diamond markers show real archive captures. Each table row opens the timestamped archived page. Capture time and retrieval time are different dates. Empty months have no evidence, and no prices are interpolated.

### Choose a useful time window

Use 30 days, 90 days, 12 months or All found. An empty range offers a way back to the full evidence. Lowest observed means the lowest captured price, not a guaranteed annual low.

### Use the evidence in a current quote

Ask a seller to confirm the exact cabin, inclusions and final UK total. An archived reduction alone cannot trigger a verified-bargain badge or establish current availability.
